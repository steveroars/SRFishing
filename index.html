📖 How to Play — SR Fishing RPG

How to fish, what you can do, and every chat command
🎮 What is SR Fishing RPG?
SR Fishing RPG is a chat-driven fishing game that runs alongside the Twitch stream. Everything happens through chat commands — you fish, battle, and earn gold while you watch! This web portal lets you view your live profile, manage your Fishing Net and Aquarium Tank, buy gear, craft bait, and check the channel leaderboards in real time.
🚀 Getting Started
  1. Join the game — type !fish in Twitch chat. This creates your account and immediately casts your first line. You start with 1,000 Gold.
  2. Connect on the web — click Login with Twitch (or Set Twitch User) so this portal shows your live profile.
  3. Claim your daily — type !daily in chat every 24 hours for 1,000 Gold. There's also a 🎁 Claim Daily button in the header.
  4. Keep fishing — every !fish sends a catch to your Fishing Net. Casts have a cooldown (base 15 minutes) that gets shorter with better rods.
⌨️ Chat Commands
Command What it does
!fish Cast your line. Catches go to your Fishing Net. Respects your rod's cooldown.
!daily Claim your 1,000 Gold daily reward. Available once every 24 hours per player.
!fishbattle [gold] Battle a random Wild Tank (AI) using your healthy tank fish. Default wager is 100 Gold.
!fishbattle @user [gold] Challenge a viewer to a PvP battle. The bot tags them and they must reply !accept or !reject within 60 seconds. Declining (or timing out) cancels the battle and refunds your wager.
!accept Accept a pending PvP challenge sent to you. The battle starts instantly — winner takes the pot.
!reject Decline a pending PvP challenge. The challenger's wagered gold is refunded.
🐟 Rarity Tiers
Every !fish roll picks a fish (or trash) based on drop chances below. Higher tiers are rarer and worth far more gold. Catching a new species for the first time ever gives a +20% value bonus!
Common — 50% Uncommon — 40% Rare — 6% Legendary — 2.5% Mythical — 1.3% Divine — 0.2%
🎣 Fishing Basics
Rods: Better rods shorten your cast cooldown (Base 15 min → Standard 12 min → Golden 9 min → Divine 6 min). Only one rod is active at a time.
Baits: Auto-consume on your next cast. Standard Bait (2× odds above Common), Power Bait (2× above Uncommon), Super Bait (guarantees above Uncommon). Craft or buy them.
Net & Decay: Raw catches sit in your Fishing Net. Unpreserved fish decay over time (50% value at 24h → 25% at 84h). The Deep Freezer upgrade stops decay permanently. Sell, transfer, or craft with catches before they rot!
🐠 Aquarium Tank & Battles
Transfer: Move your best fish from the Net into your tank (capacity 3, expandable to 5) so they can fight for you.
Feed: Fish must be fed every 12 hours (1,000 Gold, or the Auto-Feeder upgrade does it for you). Hungry fish lose HP and can faint.
Revive: Fainted fish are revived with the right Recovery Med from the shop. Revived fish start at 50% HP and passively heal +10% every 30 minutes. Fainted fish that aren't revived within 5 hours are removed from the tank.
Battles: Battles are only started from Twitch chat — see the commands above. Results replay here automatically with an animated battle theater, including HP bars and the full combat log.
🛒 Shop, ⚒️ Crafting, 📖 Log & 🏆 Ranks
Upgrades Shop: Buy rods, baits, recovery meds (Common/Uncommon 2,500g · Rare/Legendary 5,000g · Mythical/Divine 8,000g), Trophy Bait (5,000g), Fish Eggs (2,000g), and permanent upgrades — Auto-Feeder (25,000g), Tank Upgrade 3→5 slots (85,000g), Fishing Net 20% extra catch (75,000g), Deep Freezer (100,000g), Fishing Vessel (350,000g).
Crafting Station: Turn spare net catches into bait — Standard Bait (4 Common fish), Power Bait (6 Uncommon fish), Super Bait (5 Rare fish).
Species Log: Track every species you've discovered, with your personal catch counts and heaviest weights.
Leaderboards: Compete for Richest, Most Catches, Trophies (channel weight records per species — stealing one pays a 10% gold bonus!), and Battles Won.
🛖 Fishing Hut & 📜 Angler's Bounties
Fishing Hut (150,000g): Unlocks a filleting station (cook dishes), a Market Stall (sell dishes), raw fish storage (10 slots, 50,000g), and cold storage for cooked dishes — Lv1 free (12h shelf life) · Lv2 24h (10,000g) · Lv3 72h (25,000g). Dishes sell for ~1.2× the value of the fish used, and recipes pull from your Net first, then hut storage.
Recipes: 3 standard recipes are always available (Smoked Fish · Angler's Fry · Bait Snacks) plus 2 daily specials that rotate every day at midnight UTC.
Bounties (1 per day): Every day the Bounty Board posts one random bounty — turn in fish, a cooked dish, or baits for a fair gold reward. You only get bounties for species you've discovered, and you can only do 1 per day, so the rewards stay worth it without breaking the economy.
Market Stall: Sell fresh dishes for gold, or save them for a dish bounty. Spoiled dishes are removed. Toggle Auto-Sell to have every cooked dish sold instantly.
💡 Pro Tips
  • Use !daily every day — it's free gold that compounds your progress.
  • Keep your tank fed and healthy so you're always ready when someone challenges you.
  • Don't hoard net catches — decay eats their value. Sell or transfer before it's too late.
  • New species are worth more — the first catch of a species ever on the channel gets a bonus.
  • Challenge wagers are held in escrow: if your opponent declines or doesn't answer in 60 seconds, your gold is refunded automatically.
